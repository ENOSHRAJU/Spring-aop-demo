Pointcut - "It tells us where we are applying advice"
Advice - "It is something we are trying to do."
         "Advice are 5 types 1. @Before 2. @After 3. @Afterthrowing 4. @AfterReturning 5. @Around"
         "@After - applies the advice whether the joinpoint (method in the class) executes successfully or not"
         "@Afterthrowing - applies advice if the joinpoint doesnt execute and get error"
         "@AfterReturning - applies advice only after successfully executing the method."
Weaving - process of connecting the place which pointcut is showing us and applying the advice there
Joinpoint - The specific method in a class which is executing (@Before, @After, @AfterThrowing, @AfterReturning)
ProceedingJoinpoint - This is exclusive for @Around advice.

Example - Lets say we are going to a dinner with your friends to enjoy with them.
          Now here where are going to "Dinner" - so dinner is pointcut
          what are going to do there eat right - so eat is the advice
          process of we going to dinner by vehicle is weaving
          what are we going to eat is joinpoint
